Layout plans get sent back for the same handful of reasons, over and over. Here are the five we see most often in Layout Scrutiny AI reports.

## 1. Setback miscalculation on irregular plots

Rectangular plots are easy. Irregular boundary plots are where setback math quietly goes wrong — especially at acute corners.

## 2. FSI computed before premium deductions

FSI is frequently calculated against the base figure without accounting for premium or fungible FSI conditions specific to the zone.

## 3. Road-width classification errors

The applicable regulations often change based on road width bands — and plans sometimes reference an outdated road-width classification.

## 4. Open-space requirements applied per-building instead of per-plot

Open space rules are commonly miscalculated when a plot contains multiple building footprints.

## 5. Missing amendment references

Regulations get amended. A plan citing a superseded clause is one of the fastest ways to trigger a resubmission.

Catching these before submission is exactly what Layout Scrutiny AI is built to do.
