Setback requirements are often assumed to be roughly uniform across India, with local exceptions. Our structural comparison suggests the variance is larger than that framing implies.

## What we compared

Setback, FSI, and open-space requirements across a sample of states, normalized against comparable plot sizes and road-width bands.

## What we found

Setback minimums varied meaningfully even between neighboring states with similar urban density, and the *structure* of the rule — flat minimum vs. road-width-indexed — differed as often as the number itself.

## Implication for Rule Search AI

A cross-state search engine can't just index numbers — it has to preserve the structure of each rule, so a comparison shows *how* a state calculates the requirement, not just what the current minimum happens to be.
